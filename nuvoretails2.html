The Paradigm Shift: SEO to LEO

This section explores the evolution from traditional Search Engine Optimization (SEO) to LLM-based Engine Optimization (LEO). It defines LEO, compares it with SEO, discusses its benefits and challenges, and provides actionable strategies for optimizing e-commerce content for Large Language Models.

SEO vs. LEO: A Comparative Overview

Understanding the distinctions between SEO and LEO is crucial for modern e-commerce strategies. While SEO focuses on search engine algorithms, LEO targets Large Language Models, emphasizing natural language, context, and E-E-A-T signals.

Feature Traditional SEO LEO (LLM-based Engine Optimization)
Primary Goal High SERP rankings. Favorable surfacing by LLMs in conversational search & AI answers.
Target Search engine algorithms. Large Language Models (ChatGPT, Gemini).
Content Focus Keywords, backlinks, technical health. Natural language, context, E-E-A-T, human-like content.
E-commerce Implication Drive traffic for direct conversion. Influence discovery in conversational AI, establish authority.
